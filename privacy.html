Life bank reader privacy notice

This private tool is used by its owner to read the owner's own personal bank balances and transactions through Enable Banking. It does not initiate payments.

The data is saved on the owner's own Mac. The owner's personal AI assistant (Claude, from Anthropic) reads it on the owner's behalf to find recurring charges and to match payments to the owner's invoices, so parts of it pass through Anthropic's service when the assistant works with it. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Enable Banking and the banks process the authorization and the requested bank data under their own notices.

The owner keeps the local data until the owner deletes it. Bank access can be withdrawn through the bank or Enable Banking. For questions or deletion requests, contact malthezimakoff@gmail.com.
